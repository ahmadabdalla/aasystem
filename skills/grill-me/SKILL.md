# Grill me

Read the [execution contract](../pilot/references/execution.md) in full and the [principle index](../principles/INDEX.md), unless already in context. Follow the baseline rules.

Reuse the caller's scope, evidence, settled choices and checkpoint. For one missing detail, ask a focused question.

Read the procedure that fits:

- Product activities and journeys: [Discovery interviews](references/discovery-interview.md).
- Resolve or stress-test a plan: [Grilling](references/grilling.md).

When terms or consequential decisions emerge, read and apply [domain-modeling](../domain-modeling/SKILL.md). Reuse it once loaded. Record agreed terms and qualifying decisions as they settle; load format references only when needed. "With docs" uses this same path. Write only within authorized scope. For read-only work, return proposed text and destinations. Keep requirements and plans in their owning brief.

Return the result, saved record paths or proposed notes to the caller, who owns synthesis and delivery. A standalone interview ends here.
