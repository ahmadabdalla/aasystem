# Delegation

## Do it or hand it off

Follow a skill yourself. Hand off only for expertise, parallel work or a clean context.

Give each parallel worker its own files, and name what they share, such as a database or a port. Invoke the [`swarm`](../../swarm/SKILL.md) skill to split coverage. Invoke the [`arena`](../../arena/SKILL.md) skill to compare approaches when the result could change an open choice. When you split work, never give an independent candidate's, judge's or verifier's job to another worker. Coupled work stays with one owner.

Check a worker's claims against its files and check output, and resolve contradictions and stale inputs. A worker saying "done" isn't acceptance.

If your host can't start a required separate agent, say so before you plan around it. Never call your own check independent.

## Reviews

An **independent discovery** review starts fresh, with no shared history. Give the reviewer the requirements, accepted constraints, the artifact and which version it is, the criteria and the commands to run. Give it no earlier conclusions, yours or anyone's, until it reports.

A **confirmation audit** may see the claims it checks, so label it as one. The first check of new work is independent discovery; rechecks after a fix can be audits.

Keep the reviewer's original findings before summarising them, and say what context it saw. A different model alone doesn't make a review independent. Don't claim model diversity you don't have.
