# Feature

Own the design and acceptance criteria. Delegate bounded implementation, inspect it, and prove the integrated feature.

1. **Ground the change.** Reuse the agreed product contract and solution record. Use [product](../../product/SKILL.md) for missing or unsettled material behavior, business rules or acceptance scenarios. For new apps, select a first usable journey, retaining shared constraints and proposed behavior's planned status. Use [how](../../how/SKILL.md) over existing affected systems and [solution](../../solution/SKILL.md) for unsettled approaches before implementation. Carry the contract and unresolved decisions into design.
2. **Design and establish proof.** Follow the [execution contract](../references/execution.md). Routine presentation edits with unchanged behavior and prescribed existing components reuse settled interaction/implementation design and establish proof directly. For other changes:
   - For requested prototype-first refinement or materially unsettled presentation, assign [UI refinement](../../ui-refinement/SKILL.md) bounded design/review scope; resume here with its inputs and completed grounding.
   - Otherwise, assign affected interactions to [Interaction Design](../../interaction-design/SKILL.md), reusing an adequate existing model.
   - Carry interaction decisions, unresolved choices and mock omissions into [architect](../../architect/SKILL.md), assigned design-only for caller usage, data shape, boundaries, alternatives and a verification plan.

   Return changed business meaning to Product and changed interaction choices to their owner. Feature retains execution; neither design assignment starts another implementation.
3. **Choose the work structure.** For nontrivial work, record blocking prerequisites, independent workstreams, shared mutable state, and the smallest safe decomposition. A prescribed presentation-only edit with unchanged behavior and existing components may remain with the lead. Give other coupled implementation to one worker. Complete blockers first. Use [swarm](../../swarm/SKILL.md) for genuinely independent slices. Keep a coupled change with one owner.
4. **Implement against the chosen design.** Supply file ownership, named data structures, invariants, accepted decisions and success criteria, including any reviewed visual reference and explicit constraints. Use [arena](../../arena/SKILL.md) for meaningful remaining implementation alternatives and independent judging; do not repeat settled architecture competitions. Record prescribed shapes and assign their implementation under the [delegation procedure](../references/execution.md#delegation). Re-ground exposed design errors and update every affected consumer of shared primitives.
5. **Verify and review.** Exercise affected user paths and side effects on the final combined artifact. Compare rendered output with any reviewed reference through [interaction review checks](../../interaction-design/references/review-checks.md); behavioral passes do not prove visual fidelity. For prescribed low-impact presentation edits, inspect final source/diff and the available rendered surface; report briefly without a bespoke checker or full phase-completion account. State failures and coverage gaps. Use [interrogate](../../interrogate/SKILL.md) before delivery for contested designs; the lead acts on supported findings and reruns affected proof.
6. **Deliver verifiable units.** Order changes so each meaningful unit has evidence. Update affected feature recipes. Follow the execution contract's delivery boundary, including [Opening a PR](opening-a-pr.md) when authorized.

Report the behavior delivered, significant design choices and tradeoffs, work decomposition where material, verification, and unresolved decisions.
