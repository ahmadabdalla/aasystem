# Approved principles

These are portable personal judgments. While grounding a decision, match its situation to this index and read the full leaf for each principle you apply. An empty match requires no additional work. Reconsider relevance when the decision changes.

Apply a principle within its scope alongside current instructions, project requirements, and evidence. Surface a material conflict. Facts remain subject to observation and research; a preference does not establish them. In the existing decision or design rationale, identify the choice a relevant principle changed and any consequential exception. Explain that effect to the user when it matters to the recommendation.

Carry relevant leaves and their scope with the settled brief into supporting work and subsequent handoffs. Give isolated contexts the leaf content or a path they can access. Candidates receive the same applicable preferences without being coached toward a preferred answer. Preserve applied judgments and exceptions in the returned record.
