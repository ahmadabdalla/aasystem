# Engineering verification

Use within the existing [independent implementation verification](execution.md#independent-implementation-verification) gate. The root owner reads this before retained production editing or assigning proof; the verifier reads it before work. The separately scoped [owner-presentation exception](execution.md#owner-presentation-eligibility) retains its distinct proof basis and cannot waive required independent review. Keep assessment and executable claims in the same root run and verifier report; this procedure adds no specialist chain.

## Declare and assign

Before editing, the root owner declares a required engineering-assessment claim alongside executable claims in the existing [receipt fields](verification-contract.md#root-brief). Define it as completion of a bounded scope and structure review with findings and limits disclosed, not an absolute maintainability guarantee.

If a presentation run loses eligibility, apply the shared [escalation rule](execution.md#owner-presentation-eligibility) before further work, preserving current authority, effects, history, consumed budget, and proof limits without inventing human agreement. Do not omit the required assessment claim merely to reuse a run that cannot carry it.

Follow [Delegation](execution.md#delegation) and [Review context](execution.md#review-context). Assign a fresh verifier separate from every editor. Include the combined artifact's exact revision or source identity, pre-change source or diff baseline, relevant reproduction, project verifier and affected recipes, surface and side-effect expectations, environment/access limits, evidence destination, and cleanup ownership. Supply accessible Maintainability criteria: [local code](../../maintainability/references/local-code.md) for local implementation, [boundaries](../../maintainability/references/boundaries.md) for interfaces, owned knowledge, or invariant enforcement. Name the applicable scope and expected findings format from [Maintainability](../../maintainability/SKILL.md#return-a-bounded-assessment); reading criteria does not invoke another specialist.

For discovery, exclude implementation conclusions and development pass claims until the initial report; retain original requirements, constraints, contracts, and runnable recipes. Audits may receive claims and sources; disclose that mode and its limits. Give the verifier the precise return requirements below and any required receipt identity inputs; the root administers the receipt and imports metadata without changing frozen observations.

## Assess, execute, and return

The verifier inspects the actual combined diff for fit to the authorized scope and supported structural concerns using the relevant criteria, then follows [project verification](execution.md#verification-through-the-project) against the actual artifact. It changes neither product code nor expectations. Return harness drift for separate [Maintain Verification](../../maintain-verification/SKILL.md) correction; blocked product claims remain unverified.

Return each claim's pass, fail, or unverified result, observations and evidence, tested source identity, coverage and isolation limits. Keep qualitative findings and their reviewed scope separate from commands, runtime observations, and unexercised behavior. A completed assessment may report material findings; its pass means the stated review was performed and disclosed, not that every design choice is correct. Missing or inaccessible review scope remains unverified. Preserve original findings and full-context exposure evidence before lead reporting assistance; follow the assignment's snapshot, identity, and initial-return requirements. The root imports them under the receipt's [independent review rules](verification-contract.md#native-attempts-and-independent-review-evidence).

## Reconcile and accept

The owner inspects the returned artifacts and reconciles every material finding with a correction or an explicit, evidenced disposition within its authority before recording acceptance. Assign corrections through the change workflow and rerun affected independent assessment and executable proof on the current combined artifact after material changes. A candidate or verifier return is not an accepted handoff. Required missing, failing, or unverified claims block acceptance; passing review completion alone cannot waive unreconciled findings or behavioral failures. Missing delegation, runtime access, or usable proof leaves the gate unmet.

Retain finding dispositions in the explicit owner acceptance rationale and referenced evidence. Preserve the receipt's current-artifact, frozen-findings, and context-integrity requirements; receipt validation cannot judge the substance of a review or its dispositions.
