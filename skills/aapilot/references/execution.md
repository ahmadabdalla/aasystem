# Execution contract

**Baseline.** Before acting, read this contract in full, the [approved-principle index](../../principles/INDEX.md) and relevant principle leaves. Apply them within your assigned role and authorized scope. Reuse instructions already in context; every fresh worker loads its own baseline.

## Ownership and handoffs

Supporting skills return findings to the current owner. That owner consolidates requirements, terminology, consequential decisions and unresolved risks into canonical project records within scope, preserving sources, authority, rationale and evidence limits. Route reusable learning through [reflect](../../reflect/SKILL.md). Working records may stay in conversation; skill invocation alone requires no separate report. Follow the selected workflow's artifact requirements, including [show-me-your-work](../../show-me-your-work/SKILL.md)'s local log and retention rules.

Retain a separate supporting artifact only when canonical records inadequately serve an identified future reader and decision, operating procedure, verification need or audit obligation. Reuse project locations and link rather than copy evidence. Other notes and review transcripts are temporary. This does not authorize deleting existing records or evidence needed to substantiate results.

Keep owner, objective, constraints, evidence and limits, applicable principles, expected results and next action in one brief. Reuse settled grounding. Give each contributor a bounded deliverable, a work or time budget, accessible evidence and artifact paths, write ownership and a verification method; specify what it must return. Every worker, including nested delegates, must load the baseline and read its procedure and relevant project instructions before work. Reuse that baseline within its context. Give workers accessible paths, the current brief and later corrections; restrict execution to the assigned phase. The root owns receipt administration: supply workers their precise assignment and return contract without making them load the owner-only receipt cookbook. Workers return out-of-scope findings to the caller with evidence and a proposed assignment; they do not start that workflow. Helpers may compose bounded support under the same caller and checkpoint.

For time bounds, read an actual clock and normalize timestamps to one time zone; the local calendar date alone cannot establish expiry. Check remaining time and dispatch/work limits before each phase, native call or retry. If a pending tool overruns, retain the consumed or unknown budget and stop new work when control returns; do not silently extend the grant.

The lead owns framing, assignments, integration, acceptance requests and design decisions within its recorded authority. Required independent proof comes from a verifier separate from every producer of the assessed work. When claiming host-enforced verification or acceptance, apply the conditional [host acceptance contract](host-acceptance.md).

For retained production changes, use the [verification contract](verification-contract.md) by default within the selected procedure. Keep one root owner and run; assigned specialists contribute artifacts and evidence to that owner without initializing another run. Read-only answers and design-only work keep their scoped evidence obligations without a production receipt or implementation workflow.

## Scope and proof

Keep the outcome, invariants, delivery boundary, decision authority, material uncertainties and proof obligations in the existing brief. Inspect affected journeys, consumers, shared state and dependencies before confirming the execution structure. Reassess when these facts or the consequences change; retain settled grounding.

Inspect observable unknowns. Use a bounded check for technical uncertainty. Choose routine details within authority. Where reasonable interpretations produce materially different outcomes and no accepted source settles the choice, use the human checkpoint before depending on that choice. Resolve conflicting requirements through their sources and authority.

Assess reversibility of effects, affected people and systems, and actual authority together. Reverting code does not undo deleted data, external communications or customer effects. A reversible action still needs authority.

Before advancing a material claim into dependent work, identify its supporting source or observation, its status and limits, and the check or decision needed at this stage. Reuse requirements, scenarios and decision records; create no parallel claim registry.

For ideation and discovery, distinguish sourced needs from hypotheses and inspect evidence that could contradict consequential assumptions. For product definition, check scenarios, invariants and decision authority. For interaction and design, inspect journey coverage, failure and recovery states, and required human review. For retained implementation, apply the [engineering proof rules](#independent-implementation-verification). An accepted proposal does not prove demand, usability or runtime behavior.

If required evidence is missing, obtain it within scope or report the gap. Research and design may end with a conditional recommendation when that is the requested result. An unresolved prerequisite blocks dependent implementation or acceptance; an unrelated successful check cannot discharge it.

## Decision authority

Before implementation, identify consequential choices that establish or change product workflows or acceptance criteria, external contracts or data exposure, component responsibilities, state ownership or dependency directions, authority or trust boundaries, deployment or rollback, irreversible effects, or required visual review. Check whether the current request or accepted project records settle each applicable choice and identify its source; an explicit request can settle the choice. Return unresolved choices through the human checkpoint below. A request to implement an outcome does not approve choices it leaves unspecified; a model recommendation or assumption is not human agreement. Use [Grill Me](../../grill-me/SKILL.md) only for several linked choices.

At each human checkpoint, lead the caller-visible response with the artifact stage (prototype, design proposal or maintained work) and the decision awaiting the human. State what was checked and remains unverified, and link an identifiable, inspectable artifact. For visual decisions, provide a usable preview or retained rendered views with access instructions; name cleanup ownership when an active preview needs it. For nonvisual decisions, provide the relevant evidence. State the next action authorized within the current scope after the answer, then end with the exact focused question or concrete options; include consequences and a recommendation when useful for the decision. Put these essentials in the response itself; a linked package cannot replace the handoff. Stop the whole process and wait for the actual human answer. Reuse the existing project record and any applicable verification-contract package/response binding; agent inspection does not establish human viewing or acceptance.

Keep sourced requirements, delegated implementation proposals, and actual human agreements distinct in the existing brief or decision record. Record the agreed option and its answer/source reference before proceeding; a worker or receipt field cannot supply human agreement. Routine implementation details that preserve settled behavior and boundaries proceed within assigned authority, including private helpers, formatting, conventional visible affordances and extracting pure validators while preserving the agreed state owner. Return a contradiction or newly consequential choice to the owner before implementing it.

## Delegation

1. **Check capability.** At workflow start, identify the agent tool, delegation restrictions and access workers need. Check supporting procedures too. Use configured or permitted inherited models; do not invent diversity.
2. **Choose execution ownership.** The current owner may investigate, design, implement and maintain records by applying the selected procedures in its own context. A skill defines responsibility and method; selecting it does not itself require another agent. Delegate bounded work for a concrete benefit from independent ready work, evidence access or context separation. Preserve explicitly required independent reviews, comparisons and verification. Direct execution retains every procedure's scope, decision authority and evidence obligations.
3. **Structure the work.** For nontrivial implementation, record blocking prerequisites, independent workstreams, shared mutable state and the smallest safe decomposition. Revisit the structure when scope, dependencies, consequences or proof needs change. Complete blockers first. Keep coupled work with one execution owner; parallelize independent ready assignments when their benefit justifies the handoff and actual limits permit it. Sequence work when slots are occupied. Workers unable to spawn return required supporting assignments to their caller.
4. **Accept results.** Inspect artifacts and evidence against completion criteria before advancing. Make bounded corrections within current authority or assign them with the updated brief; return changed design or authority to its owner. Self-review cannot satisfy a required independent gate; keep it incomplete when independence cannot be achieved.

Use bounded subagents for large documents, verbose outputs, screenshots or broad exploration when separate coverage or context separation provides a concrete benefit. Give them artifact pointers and the consolidated brief; request concise findings, evidence paths and gaps. For direct work, narrow reads and save bulky output to files.

For each required independent assessment, name the affected domains, governing criteria and expected evidence in the assignment. Give the relevant specialist procedures to a verifier separate from every producer of the assessed work, following [review context](#review-context). One capable verifier may cover related domains; add specialists only when required separate coverage needs them. Uncovered assessment remains unverified.

Use [swarm](../../swarm/SKILL.md) for partitioned work or coverage and [arena](../../arena/SKILL.md) for competing alternatives and synthesis. Use a fresh worker for required independent verification or review and for assignments needing isolation. A bounded correction may stay with its current implementer when scope and authority remain valid and independence is not required. Reassign when changed scope, missing capability or repeated failure makes a different context necessary. Preserve earlier findings, consumed budget and actual native followup receipts. Transfer the consolidated brief and corrections; select review inputs under the [review-context rule](#review-context). Tell workers they share the codebase, must preserve others' changes and return actual artifacts and evidence. Give parallel writers separate artifacts or explicitly nonoverlapping sections; serialize shared canonical sections. Before integration, check each returned brief and source revision against current task/project records, including the latest assigned proposal. Preserve proposed versus accepted status; recheck stale or conflicting findings.

For an additional execution worker, record the concrete reason in the existing assignment: an independent ready deliverable, a required evidence/tool capability, or a context-separation need. Name dependencies, write ownership, return evidence and integration responsibility. A skill name, file count or apparent task size alone does not establish that reason. Required independent gates remain mandatory.

For competing alternatives, identify the unresolved choice, materially different plausible options and the evidence independent candidates could add. Apply the owning comparison procedure; retain Arena's isolated candidates and separate judging when Arena is selected. Reuse an adequate settled decision unless changed evidence warrants reopening it.

Workflow configuration does not authorize unavailable tools or external actions.

## Review context

Label review assignments as independent discovery or confirmation/evidence audit. An open search for weaknesses uses discovery; checking specified claims uses an audit.

For independent discovery, supply the objective, authoritative requirements, accepted constraints, source identity, original artifacts, and evidence/access limits. Exclude lead conclusions, suggested answers, earlier reviewer findings, and inherited discussion. Keep these inputs and the original artifact intact. Record exposure to prior assessments from assignment through the initial returned findings, including exposure through artifacts, host context, messages, or reporting assistance. Disclose it in the review report.

Start each reviewer without the parent conversation. If the host cannot exclude it, report which prior context the reviewer receives. Retain each reviewer’s initial findings before lead help with formatting or receipt metadata, and preserve that initial version. Do not share findings between reviewers until all initial reports return. Then compare the reports against evidence. A fresh worker or different model alone does not establish independent discovery. Report isolation limits.

For a confirmation/evidence audit, supply claims and sources, authoritative requirements and original evidence. Treat claims as hypotheses. Derive expected results from requirements, examine supporting and contradictory evidence, and report supported, contradicted, or inconclusive results. Audit agreement does not establish independent discovery or satisfy a gate that requires it.

For independent corroboration of a lead conclusion, collect and retain initial discovery reports before revealing it. A confirmation/evidence audit needs no preceding discovery review. Model diversity is optional.

## Verification through the project

Read authoritative product requirements and any active change; preserve sources, status, invariants and unresolved decisions. For OpenSpec projects or requested adoption, read the [OpenSpec reference](openspec.md) within your role.

Read the relevant project glossary and current ADRs. Carry accepted constraints and unresolved assumptions into the task brief; surface contradictions before changing behavior. Use [domain-modeling](../../domain-modeling/SKILL.md) to clarify or record them when needed.

Before product changes, locate the verifier and feature map; identify affected outcomes, alternate entry points, dependent callers and coverage gaps. If no usable proof path exists, use [bootstrap-verification](../../bootstrap-verification/SKILL.md) within scope. A scoped verifier can suffice without whole-project setup. Edits meeting [owner-presentation eligibility](#owner-presentation-eligibility) use existing source/diff checks and an available preview; a missing formal verifier alone does not require scaffolding. Unexercised appearance and behavior remain unverified. For design-only work, record missing proof without starting implementation.

Before editing, derive expected results from requirements or the contract. Exercise the real UI, CLI, API or library entry point and inspect side effects. Follow verifier readiness, instance identity, isolation, reset, evidence and cleanup procedures. Recheck health and reset state before interpreting surprising results. Mocks, builds and worker summaries cannot prove unexercised boundaries.

Follow project evidence procedures and the [evidence contract](../../bootstrap-verification/references/project-contract.md#evidence-and-retention) for claim strength, run locations, review retention and disposal. Distinguish harness faults, product defects and missing access. Use [maintain-verification](../../maintain-verification/SKILL.md) for recipe drift. Never change expectations to hide regressions; record authority for intentional changes. Update and recheck affected feature recipes when behavior or procedure changes; unchanged runs need no permanent map edit.

Verify each meaningful unit and the final combined artifact under the [independent implementation verification procedure](#independent-implementation-verification) when applicable. Clean up only owned resources; confirm evidence survives. Identify unverified claims in partial results.

## Owner-presentation eligibility

The root owner may execute and check an exact prescribed, low-impact presentation edit within existing components when meaning, behavior, data, contracts, permissions, dependencies and deployment remain unchanged, and required design, authority and human checkpoints are settled. This exception cannot waive an independent engineering review required by the user, project, or task. Word count alone establishes nothing: legal, pricing or consent wording can change business meaning.

Record the eligibility basis before editing, then inspect the actual diff against it before acceptance. Use the common [verification receipt](verification-contract.md) with nonempty specific claims, current artifact identity, actual source/diff observations, an available preview and disclosed unexercised claims. Label this owner-presentation proof; it never establishes independent verification. Reuse the request, project evidence and owner report, generating routine metadata through the helper. No worker assignment, preflight, frozen review packet, bespoke checker or full phase checklist is required.

If actual effects exceed eligibility, stop owner-presentation acceptance and strengthen proof obligations before proceeding. Use the same run only within existing authority, immutable resource IDs and remaining budget. Otherwise return the limit and required decision to the human and wait for authorized continuation. When immutable root identity requires a linked new run, preserve prior history, effects, checkpoints, uncertain effects, deadlines and consumed nonrefundable budget; never reset them to obtain eligibility.

## Independent implementation verification

For other retained production implementation, the owner dispatches a fresh verifier separate from every editor to both assess the actual diff and execute affected checks; development checks cannot satisfy this gate. The [owner-presentation exception](#owner-presentation-eligibility) retains its distinct proof basis. Design-only and throwaway experiments retain their own evidence scope.

The root owner reads [engineering verification](engineering-verification.md) before editing or assigning that proof; the assigned verifier reads it before assessment and execution. It owns the predeclared assessment claim, bounded assignment, distinct qualitative and runtime evidence, and finding reconciliation before explicit owner acceptance. Keep one root run and current-artifact proof under the [receipt contract](verification-contract.md). Other workers return their assigned artifacts and development evidence; they do not administer this gate. [Interrogate](../../interrogate/SKILL.md) provides adversarial review, not executable verification.

## Completion

Plan applicable decision reconciliation, verification maintenance and evidence retention in the existing task list, with owners and completion criteria. Delegate under the procedure above.

Before reporting completion, check required results, reconcile consequential findings into canonical records, and verify affected references. Report incomplete assignments and unmet gates within the requested scope and delivery boundary.

## Bounded autonomy

Before authorized unattended work, reuse the existing brief to establish the outcome and required evidence, invariants, permitted choices and effects, delivery boundary, practical budget, and stopping/fallback behavior. Use actual user instructions and accepted project records as authority. A duration such as overnight establishes no additional permission.

Proceed with routine decisions within those bounds. An explicit advance instruction may settle a consequential choice or its permitted range; preserve its source and limits. A model-selected preference or an unspecified reversible action does not settle an unresolved consequential choice.

Resolve observable and technical uncertainty within the declared budget. Stop when a required human decision, unresolved authority conflict, unavailable mandatory proof, exhausted limit or uncertain external effect prevents authorized continuation. Reconcile uncertain effects under the existing receipt procedure. Do not silently expand scope or reset consumed budget.

At a human checkpoint, stop the whole process and wait. Continuing independent units past that checkpoint remains outside this procedure. Report completed, failed, unverified and blocked outcomes with current artifacts, evidence, consumed or unknown budget, and the next required decision or observation.

For production runs, map these bounds into existing authority sources, actions/resources, constraints, expected outputs, next action, checkpoints and budget fields. Research/design work retains its existing scoped records and evidence. Add no scheduler, persistent runner, recovery promise or universal receipt for non-production work.

## Delivery

Follow the requested delivery boundary and repository conventions. Local edits, commits, PR creation, monitoring and merging are distinct actions. Use [Opening a PR](../playbooks/opening-a-pr.md) when authorized. For commits, retain failing-before evidence and verifiable change order; never rewrite shared history to manufacture it. Read-only work ends with its answer.
